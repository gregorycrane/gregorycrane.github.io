printed p. 433 · epigram

68.—On the Same

Escort divine Nonna from the church, all ye people, escort the grand old woman raised on high.

printed p. 433 · epigram

69.—On the Same

God from his pure temple took to heaven Nonna eager to join the heavenly ones.

printed p. 433 · epigram

70.—On the Same

Nonna rising from the great church said “I have a reward greater than all my many labours.”

printed p. 433 · epigram

71.—On the Same

Here lies Nonna, victim of a pure prayer. Here Nonna while praying was released from life.

printed p. 433 · epigram

72.—On the Same

Here Nonna’s soul left her body while she was praying. Hence Nonna leaving her body was taken up.

printed p. 433 · epigram

73.—On the Same

Nonna rose, a great sacrifice, from the great church. In the church Nonna died. Rejoice all ye pious.

74.—On the Same

This altar sent God-like Nonna to God.
